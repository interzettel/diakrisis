## Sources checked for this revision

- [Calvo Figueras & Agerri (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11341v3): critical questions generated by LLMs are often generic, templated or bring in concepts absent from the text; no model exceeded about 57% useful questions zero-shot.
- [Merits and defects of LLM review generation (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.19326): LLM reviews describe strengths well but produce far fewer weaknesses and barely track paper quality.
- [Li et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.25415): LLM reviewers inflate ratings and confidence and can be steered by hidden instructions in the reviewed text.
- [Zhao et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.07723): non-existent references rose sharply after 2023; whether a real source supports a claim is harder to check than whether it exists.
- [Sjøberg & Bergersen (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05336): construct validity is distinct from internal, external and statistical-conclusion validity; basis for the method boundary.

## Decisions

- **Adopt categories:** `unsupported-inference`, `overgeneralization`, `causal-overclaim`, `missing-warrant`, `unacknowledged-alternative`, `certainty-mismatch`, `source-support-gap` (a real source does not support the claim), `unverifiable-source` (the source could not be inspected).
- **Boundary with method:** a defect in how a study was designed or measured goes to method-review; a conclusion that exceeds what the reported design supports stays here. Both may apply as two related findings with different causes.
- **Boundary with communication:** whether confidence language matches the evidence (`certainty-mismatch`) belongs here; communication-review only flags hedging that is ambiguous about the author's actual confidence.
- **Replace the shared worked example.** Argument-review and method-review both used "fewer geometric violations, therefore better architecture". Argument keeps an overgeneralization example from a different domain; method keeps a measurement example.
- **Inline** the category list and the severity definitions in `SKILL.md`, matching the contract (spec: references relative to the skill root).
- **Reject:** importing critical-question templates; they are what makes LLM questions generic.

## Controls

- Positive: "A survey of 40 students in one city shows remote work raises productivity in every industry" → `overgeneralization`.
- Negative: "We hypothesise that independent review may help; this is untested" → no finding.
- Abstention: the decisive source is paywalled and not supplied → `unverifiable-source` with `abstain`, never an invented contradiction.
