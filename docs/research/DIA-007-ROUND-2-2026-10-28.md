# DIA-007 round 2: revised skills on the owner's v7.6 proposal (28 Oct 2026)

**Status:** results of one exploratory round. It shows a direction, not an effect. Nothing here validates a skill or accepts a milestone.

**Publication:** aggregate counts only, the scope the owner approved for round 1 on 28 Oct 2026. The document, findings, quotes and grades stay private.

## Design

The protocol is [EVALUATION.md](../EVALUATION.md), registered before the runs (SHA-256 `956975dd…`).

- **Skills.** The 2026-10-28 revision at `28067ca` ([SUITE-REVISION-2026-10-28.md](SUITE-REVISION-2026-10-28.md)).
- **Material.** The owner's revised research proposal (v7.6), frozen as Markdown. The 8-word overlap with the skill and fixture files was zero.
- **Cells.** Six review types, run once per arm: 12 runs, at most 6 findings each, giving 70 findings (35 per arm).
- **Isolation.** The reviewers had no access to the repository holding round 1 findings or grades.
- **Blinding.** Findings were pooled and shuffled with the arm key sealed (SHA-256 `c7bc3027…`). The key opened after both graders had frozen a verdict for every ID.
- **Graders.**
  - ChatGPT (Codex): a different model family from the reviewers, and project-informed.
  - The owner's Claude assistant: a co-author of v7.6, the same family as the reviewers.

## Pre-specified checks of the revision

- **Mandatory extra arm.** The only finding that demanded a planned replay arm came from the baseline. One grader rejected it outright. The skill arm's finding on the same design gap offered "plan it, or state the limit", and a grader named that as the fair remedy.
- **Construct substitution.** The finding proposing to replace the principal outcome with an easier measure came from the baseline. Both graders rejected the substitution.
- **Compatible role description.** Both arms now describe the owner's role credit and the public record as compatible. The baseline also improved, so this cannot be credited to the revision.
- **Silence is not contradiction.** The one finding the owner's later confirmation contradicts came from the baseline (see Owner fact).

## Consolidation (secondary)

A separate agent per arm merged that arm's findings into problem families. The skill arm used the revised coordinator; the baseline used a plain instruction.

- Both produced 23 families from 35 findings.
- Both placed every same-arm duplicate pair the graders had marked into one family: 6 of 6 and 3 of 3 for one grader, 4 of 4 and 6 of 6 for the other.
- This measure does not separate the arms.

## Comparison with round 1

{expert inference}

- The direction is the same: more agree and fewer rejected findings with the skill.
- The cross-family grader's gap is small: 6 vs 3 agree, 15 vs 20 partly.
- The round 1 failure modes that the revision targeted appeared only in the baseline this round.
- One run per cell cannot separate a skill effect from run-to-run variation.

## Deviations

1. **Corpus artifact.** The frozen Markdown source lacks the PDF's Figure 1, so a baseline finding that the figure is missing is an artifact of the evaluator's setup, not a manuscript defect.
2. **Co-author grader.** One grader is a co-author of the manuscript.

## Limits

{expert inference}

- One run per cell.
- Both graders are project-informed AI assistants, not human gold.
- v7.6 was written from round 1's findings, so it is a cleaner manuscript, but not a naive one.
- Recall was not measured.
