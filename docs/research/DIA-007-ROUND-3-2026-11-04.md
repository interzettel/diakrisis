# DIA-007 round 3: skill × input format on owner material (28 Oct to 4 Nov 2026)

**Status:** results of one exploratory round. It is evidence of direction, not of an effect. Nothing here validates a skill or accepts a milestone.

**Publication:** the owner approved publishing these aggregate counts on 4 Nov 2026. The document, findings, quotes and grades stay private.

## Design

Registered before any run (protocol SHA-256 `834a7def…`), under [docs/EVALUATION.md](../EVALUATION.md).

- **Material.** The owner's revised doctoral research proposal (v7.7), five typeset pages with one vector figure, frozen in a private repository. Two inputs: the PDF itself, and its `pdftotext` extraction. The 8-word overlap with every skill file and fixture was zero.
- **Skills under test.** Diakrisis at `402f26f` (the [2026-10-28 revision](SUITE-REVISION-2026-10-28.md)).
- **Design.** 2 × 2: arm (skill vs no-skill baseline) × format (PDF vs text), six review types each, one run per cell: 24 runs, cap 6 findings.
- **Consolidation.** One coordinator pass per arm and format grouped that version's findings into problem families before any verdict was seen.
- **Blinding.** All 133 findings pooled, shuffled and posted to the owner's thread; arm and format sealed (key SHA-256 `57c2d873…`), opened after both graders had frozen a verdict on every ID.

The largest family was one problem reported 16 times, four times in every version, by four different review types. Most within-version repeats paired terminology review with consistency, communication or conceptual-model review, or argument review with method review.

**Consolidation against grader duplicates.** In every version and for both graders, the coordinator's families grouped every within-version pair the grader marked as duplicate (8 to 13 pairs per version). Of the pairs the families grouped, 2 to 4 per version were not marked duplicate by the grader.

**Format-specific tags.** Ten findings carried a tag: eight `(figure)`, spread over all four versions, and two `(extraction)`, both from text versions, which quoted a hyphenated compound as the extractor had joined it.

**Secondary outcomes:**

- **Quote check** (against the text extraction): text versions 57 found, 8 found-normalized, 0 missing; PDF versions 39 found, 27 found-normalized, 2 missing. Both "missing" quotes were correct quotes of the PDF; the extraction had dropped a hyphen. `tools/check_spans.py` now reports these as `found-dehyphenated` (PR #57).
- **Contract reports.** All 12 skill-arm reports validated against the revision 2 schema.

## Reading

{expert inference}

- **Format.** PDF and text inputs performed about the same. PDF versions covered one to three more problems, which one run per cell cannot separate from noise. Figure findings came from both formats because the extraction keeps the caption. The skills are not restricted to PDF input.
- **Arm.** The co-author grader endorsed more problems from the skill versions; the cross-family grader's counts were close. The two graders' agree/partly threshold differs, so their counts are not comparable with each other.
- **Duplicates** are a property of the raw specialist output (overlapping review scopes under a finding cap), not of the consolidated report.
- **Remaining errors.** A record that described less than the owner's account was still reported as a conflict (both arms), and skill-arm findings judged cited sources from titles or abstracts. Both feed the [2026-11-04 revision](SUITE-REVISION-2026-11-04.md).

## Deviations from the registered protocol

1. **Co-author grader.** One grader is a co-author of the manuscript, as in round 2.
2. **Graders consulted owner facts.** Both graders used facts the owner had confirmed in their project, which the reviewers did not have. Verdicts on record-comparison findings reflect that knowledge.

## Limits

{expert inference} One run per cell; both graders are project-informed and one shares the reviewers' model family; the agree/partly boundary is a judgement call; recall was not measured, because the owner's known-problem list was not collected.
