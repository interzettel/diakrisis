# Communication review: source review before implementation

**Observed:** 2026-10-26. **Scope:** Syntax, discourse semantics, pragmatics and audience adaptation. **Gate:** pre-implementation design only; model testing explicitly deferred by owner. **Source policy:** inspect linked primary materials where public; do not claim access to paid standards or validation from cited material.

## Checked external sources

- [Microsoft Writing Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide): domain-specific technology writing recommendations for clarity and terminology, not a universal research writing authority.
- [Microsoft brand voice guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/brand-voice-above-all-simple-human): specificity, audience and readability; optional inspiration, not a requirement to imitate the Microsoft brand.
- [EQUATOR reporting resources](https://equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/index.html): scientific communication includes disclosure of research design; reporting completeness is distinct from methods validity.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): optional writing references and focused skill triggering help preserve context without bloating every turn.

## Comparison with repo and donors

Writing Kitchen is the intended owner of drafting and voice preferences; Diakrisis should produce **feedback and optional alternatives**, not silently rewrite author work. Terminology-review catches domain word-definition mismatch, while communication-review covers syntactic ambiguity, discourse flow, readership, structure, implicit claims and pragmatics. Vale can provide deterministic style lint where local user policy explicitly requests it. Do not import generic brand voice as compulsory policy.

## Decisions

- Always identify language, audience, genre and desired author voice; no default anglicization of German/Turkish, no homogenization of original stylistic choices.
- Flag sentence scope ambiguities that change interpretation; separate grammar errors from deliberate creative fragments.
- Quote small exact passages and explain reader consequence, not “sounds bad” or excessive rewrite proposals.
- Distinguish clear but scientifically ungrounded statements: route evidence defects to argument review.

## Challenge cases and handoff

Positive: sentence where restrictive clause ambiguity changes which agents completed a review. Negative: an intentionally fragmented literary passage that is coherent for its audience. Unknown: cross-cultural idiom with unspecified readership → ask scope, propose options. **Behavior evaluation deferred.**
